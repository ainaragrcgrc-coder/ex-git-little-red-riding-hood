## Brand & Style

This design system channels the warmth, wonder, and subtle drama of an illustrated fairy-tale anthology. Built for an immersive digital reading experience of classic folklore, it blends romantic editorial design with tactile print sensibilities. The atmosphere feels archival yet lively—evoking deckled parchment, crimson wool cloaks, and misty woodland paths.

The target audience spans discerning readers, educators, children, and parents seeking an artful, distraction-free literary medium. The emotional response should be inviting, timeless, nostalgic, and gently suspenseful.

The visual style is **Tactile Editorial**:
- Rich, physical-world metaphors echoing cloth-bound volumes, gold-embossed chapter headings, and illuminated drop caps.
- Delicate borders reminiscent of vintage book framing and woodblock prints.
- Intentional, measured pacing through spacious typographic hierarchy, avoiding cold technological modernism in favor of organic warmth.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural and symbolic palette of the forest path:

- **Primary (`#A8201A` - Cloak Scarlet):** An iconic, saturated crimson representing Little Red Riding Hood's wool mantle. Used for primary calls to action, focal chapter headings, drop caps, and key interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#264633` - Deep Forest Green):** A grounded evergreen tone inspired by pine needles and deep woods. Used for secondary navigation, pill badges, active selections, and grounding structural elements.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` - Antique Gold Leaf):** A warm gilded accent reminiscent of aged book foil. Used sparingly for decorative divider florets, highlight badges, and focus rings.
- **Neutral (`#382923` - Rich Bark Brown):** An earthy, deep umber that replaces pure harsh black for all primary text, icons, and defined framing rules.
- **Background & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF6EE` (Warm Parchment)
  - Card/Paper Surface: `#FFFDF9` (Pressed Cream Paper)
  - Muted Overlay / Shading: `#F0E7D8` (Aged Page Tint)
  - Border Hairlines: `#E2D5C3` (Weathered Page Edge)

## Typography

Typography functions as the voice of the narrator. 

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys traditional European storybook elegance with pronounced stroke contrast, classical bracketed serifs, and high-impact italics. Used for book titles, chapter headers, character introductions, and pull quotes.
- **Body & Captions (Merriweather):** Chosen for its stout serifs, generous x-height, and superior on-screen legibility. Designed to endure long reading sessions without eye fatigue against the warm cream background.
- **Micro-Copy & Labels:** Transformed via uppercase styling and wide letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror classical typeset inscriptions on spine labels and chapter vignettes.
- **Drop Caps:** First paragraphs of chapters feature a 4-line inset display drop cap styled in `Playfair Display`, rendered in Scarlet Red with an optional subtle gold leaf outline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a fixed-max-width editorial reading column within an adaptive grid, mimicking the proportions of an open octavo volume.

- **Reading Spine:** Prose flows within `page-max-reading` (approx. 65–75 characters per line) to ensure optimal reading ergonomics.
- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with 32px gutters and variable outer margins, capped at `72rem` (1152px) for structured application interfaces, centered in the viewport.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px page margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 16px side margins.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px modular scale. Generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) between narrative sections mimics the physical breathing room of printed page turns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows or cold glass effects. Instead, depth is achieved through layered paper surfaces, warm ambient halos, and framed enclosures:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF6EE` (Warm Parchment background).
  - **Level 1 (Deckled Page):** `#FFFDF9` (Raised paper panel) paired with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E2D5C3` and a soft, warm umber halo: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(56, 41, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(56, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 2 (Popovers & Modals):** `#FFFDF9` backed by a double border (1px outer `#D4A359`, 1px gap, 1px inner `#E2D5C3`) and an amber-tinted shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(56, 41, 35, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(56, 41, 35, 0.06)`.
- **Ornamental Frames:** Vignettes and hero cards feature a delicate inner keyline inset by 4px to 6px, evoking traditional bookplate margins.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Soft (`1`)** geometry. Rather than generic ultra-rounded corners or clinical razor edges, subtle corner softening (4px on interactive controls, 8px on cards and sheets) evokes hand-trimmed paper, book leaves, and bound leather corners.

- **Buttons & Inputs:** `4px` corner radius (`rounded-sm`).
- **Cards & Story Panels:** `8px` corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Badges & Chapter Tabs:** Full pill rounding (`rounded-full`) used exclusively for category ribbons and audio playback indicators to create tactile contrast against rectangular book blocks.
- **Dividers & Flourishes:** Section breaks utilize ornamental SVG glyphs—such as floral fleuron motifs, stylized oak leaves, or diamond rules—in `#D4A359`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Cloak Scarlet (`#A8201A`) with Ivory text (`#FFFDF9`), `rounded-sm` (4px), padded `12px 24px`. Subtly raises on hover with a warm shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(168, 32, 26, 0.25)` and deepens to `#8E1B16`.
- **Secondary:** Outlined in Forest Green (`#264633`) with a 1.5px border over transparent cream background; text in `#264633`. On hover, fills with Forest Green tint (`rgba(38, 70, 51, 0.08)`).
- **Text/Ghost:** Inset underlined link in Rich Bark Brown (`#382923`) with an animated Scarlet Red underline on hover.

### Cards & Narrative Folios
- Background in Pressed Cream (`#FFFDF9`), bounded by a 1px border in `#E2D5C3`.
- Inset decorative border on featured cards (4px inset line in `#D4A359` at 40% opacity).
- Soft umber hover lift with an ambient shadow transition.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact tags with pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 12px`.
- Styled in Deep Forest Green background (`#264633`) with Parchment text (`#FAF6EE`), or light parchment tone (`#F0E7D8`) with Bark Brown text (`#382923`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFDF9` background, 1px border in `#D4A359`, text in `#382923`. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px Cloak Scarlet (`#A8201A`) ring with a 2px offset in `#FAF6EE`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom square/circle controls bounded by `#264633`. Checked state fills with `#A8201A` featuring an antique off-white checkmark icon.

### Lists & Bookmarks
- Bullet points replaced with custom mini acorn, leaf, or diamond florets in Antique Gold (`#D4A359`).
- Chapter lists styled with dotted leader rules connecting chapter names on the left to page numbers on the right.

### Storybook Specifics
- **Pull Quotes:** Centered between double hairline rules, rendered in italicized `Playfair Display`, with deep scarlet opening and closing quotation marks.
- **Audio/Narration Bar:** Floating bottom sheet with deckled edges, wood-tone controls, and antique gold volume sliders.
